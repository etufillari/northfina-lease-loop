## Responsibility

Owns all pure, deterministic business logic for Lease Loop. Every calculation, rule evaluation, and state-transition decision is expressed here as a plain function with a co-located Vitest suite (`*.test.ts`).

- **Implemented rules:**
  - `coefficient.ts` (`monthlyFactorFor`): Resolves the financing band (`A`–`D`, inclusive cent bounds) and monthly factor for a financed cent amount and term (`12`, `24`, `36`, `48` months), returning `{ ok: false, reason: 'below_minimum' | 'above_maximum' | 'unknown_term' }` when out of bounds.
  - `price-list.ts` (`listPriceOn`, `effectiveAssetPriceOn`): Resolves an asset's net list price and price-list version on a given `YYYY-MM-DD` date (`valid_from <= isoDate` and `valid_to === null || isoDate <= valid_to`), using `relist_price_cents` when an asset has been relisted for a second or third life.
  - `contract-pricing.ts` (`calculateContractQuote`, `vatRateOn`, `downPaymentBoundsFor`, `clampDownPaymentCents`, `availableTermsFromTable`): Quote from list price, term and down payment (monthly net rent, VAT, buyout, totals) and VAT lookup by date.
  - `money.ts` (`formatEuro`, `parseEuroToCents`): Formats integer euro cents into localized currency strings at the UI edge and parses user-entered euro filter strings into integer cents.
  - `catalogue-filter.ts` (`filterCatalogue`, `parseCatalogueParams`): Pure filtering of catalogue items by category, accent-insensitive free-text query, asset status, and min/max list price in cents, plus safe URL query-parameter parsing.
  - `date-input.ts` (`daysInMonth`, `parseDateParts`, `splitIsoDate`): Pure calendar validation and conversion between day/month/year fields and ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` dates at the UI edge.
  - `question-flow.ts`: `evaluateCondition` / `isAskable` evaluate `ask_when` (all conditions must hold; `in` compares as text, `gte`/`lte` are inclusive and numeric only; an unknown value never satisfies a condition). `visibleQuestions` returns the applicable questions in bank order and ignores the stored answer of any question that is itself hidden. `pruneAnswers` returns `{ kept, dropped }` after an earlier answer or the deal changes; cascades resolve in one pass and answers for unknown ids are dropped too.
  - `business-id.ts` (`isValidBusinessId`): Finnish Y-tunnus format and check digit (weights 7, 9, 10, 5, 8, 4, 2; remainder 1 is never valid).
  - `eligibility.ts` (`decideEligibility`, `ageOn`): turns answers into `yes`, `no`, `manual_review` or `incomplete` (with the missing question ids). Reasons are `{ rule, outcome, params }`, never prose, so the UI translates them. A hard `no` wins immediately, even while other questions are unanswered; then `incomplete`; then `manual_review`. Rules: `min_age` (on the contract start date), `residency`, `affordability` (rent incl. VAT × 100 > income × 30, integers only; skipped when the caller passes no rent or the income question was not asked), `business_id`, `pep_manual_review`, `financed_limits`. Two ids go beyond the data pack: `signatory_required` (applicant cannot sign for a company: manual review) and `blocked_answer` (any `answer_in` block other than the country). Income answers are whole cents.
  - `invoice-schedule.ts` (`isLeapYear`, `daysInMonth`, `addMonthsClamped`, `formatInvoiceNumber`, `generateFinnishReferenceNumber`, `generateInvoiceSchedule`): Pure calendar date math with month-end clamping (`Jan 31` → `Feb 28` / `Feb 29` on leap years), deterministic invoice document ID and modulo-10 (weights 7, 3, 1) Finnish reference number generation, and monthly invoice generation that locks contract net rent while resolving VAT dynamically on each invoice's due date (`isVatChangedFromSigning`).
  - `end-of-term.ts` (`calculateBuyoutSettlement`, `interpolateResidualCurve`, `calculateResidualMarketValue`, `evaluateSecondLifeCandidates`, `matchSecondLifeWaitingList`, `monthsBetweenIso`, `deriveAssetSecondLifeValuation`, `calculateAvoidedCo2`): Pure end-of-term and second-life logic covering buyout settlements with dynamic VAT on settlement date, residual curve interpolation with condition and usage adjustments (validated on Card 9), deterministic second-life waiting-list matchmaking (returning both ranked eligible matches and excluded candidates with exclusion reasons), full second-life asset valuation and relist eligibility checks, and circular avoided manufacturing CO₂ calculation.
  - `early-exit.ts` (`billedMonthsAsOf`, `outstandingBalanceCents`, `calculateEarlyExit`): Fair early exit (Smart Problem 2, card 8), designed in `ARCHITECTURE.md` §7. The outstanding balance is the remaining rents plus the end-of-term buyout, each discounted at the band's monthly rate; with no rents left it equals the buyout exactly. The customer can buy it out (pay the balance) or return it (pay `max(0, min(balance − market value, remaining rents))`, the market value coming from `calculateResidualMarketValue` at an age of the rent periods started). The present value is computed exactly as one BigInt fraction (the annual rate in whole basis points, so `1 + r = (120000 + bp) / 120000`) and rounded half up once; the "interest not charged" line is derived, so every option's `lines` add up to its net amount. VAT follows the exit date through `calculateBuyoutSettlement`. Throws `RangeError` for an exit date before the lease starts. `earlyExitForContract` prices an exit from a signed `ContractQuote`, an exit month (clamped to the term) and a condition; the invoices page and the early-exit route both use it.
  - `reservation.ts` (`isReservationExpired`, `calculateReservationExpiry`, `remainingReservationSeconds`, `formatRemainingTime`, `canReserveAsset`, `shouldReleaseReservationOnDecision`): Pure rules for asset reservation timeouts, expiration checks, session continuity, concurrent reservation conflict detection, and decision-driven reservation release.
  - `offer.ts` (`calculateOfferExpiryDate`, `isOfferExpired`, `remainingOfferDays`, `evaluateOfferPriceProtection`, `calculateQuoteWithOffer`): Pure logic for longer-term offers (default 14-day validity, presets 7/14/30 days) and price protection. Evaluates price list increases between offer creation and customer agreement to lock the lower offer price, calculates exact list price and monthly rent savings, and handles price decreases per contract terms while reverting expired offers to current catalogue pricing.
- **Target Spine & Smart Problem engines (not yet implemented):**
  - `end-of-term`: Contract extension.
  - `second-life`: Mandatory/optional refurbishment cost evaluation (`refurbishment-costs.json`).

## Contracts and Decisions

- **Cent rounding rule:** Money is always an integer number of euro cents (`Number.isInteger(cents)`). Derived cent amounts in `contract-pricing.ts`, `invoice-schedule.ts`, and `end-of-term.ts` (`monthlyNetRentCents`, `monthlyVatCents`, `buyoutNetCents`, `vatCents`, `calculateResidualMarketValue`) are rounded once to the nearest cent with `Math.round` (half-up for positive numbers), matching `data/DATA.md` (`10730`, `2736`, `13466`, `58350`). The present-value discount in `early-exit.ts` (`outstandingBalanceCents`) converts the annual interest rate to whole basis points (`25.5` → `2550`, `7.9` → `790`) and evaluates the discounted cash flows as a single exact `BigInt` fraction rounded half up once (`roundHalfUp`). Passing a non-integer cent value to a domain function is a programming bug and throws `RangeError`.
- **Expected failures are values, not exceptions:** Domain rejections (amount below minimum, unknown asset, ineligible applicant) return discriminated unions (`{ ok: true, ... } | { ok: false, reason }`). Exceptions (`RangeError`) are thrown only for contract violations by the caller (non-integer cents or malformed `YYYY-MM-DD` strings).

## Boundary

- **Allowed imports:** Types and pure schemas from `data` (`@/data/schemas`) and sibling modules inside `src/domain/`.
- **Forbidden reaches (enforced by `eslint.config.mjs`):** Must never import `node:*`, `@/data/load`, `@/server/*`, `@/app/*`, `@prisma/*`, `@/generated/*`, `next/*`, or `react`, and must never access `fetch`, `process`, ` console`, `Date.now()`, `new Date()`, or `Math.random()`. All dates arrive as `YYYY-MM-DD` arguments from the caller.
