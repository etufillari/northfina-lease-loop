1. **Contract page, 768 and 375, every language: the page scrolls sideways (851 / 910 px at 768 in en / fi; 835 / 894 at 375).** Cause: in `src/components/invoice-table.tsx` the status cell's `Badge` renders a `sr-only` prefix ("Status: ", `src/components/ui/badge.tsx:74`). `sr-only` is `position: absolute`, and the table's scroll wrapper (`div.overflow-x-auto`, line 57) is `position: static`, so the absolutely positioned spans are placed against the page, not the wrapper, and escape its overflow clip at the table's full width (891 px). On a phone the layout viewport grows to 835 px and the whole page zooms out. Fix for the owner (invoicing/exits, on `main`): add `relative` to the wrapper's classes. The table itself scrolls correctly inside its wrapper. Not seen at 1280 (the table fits).
